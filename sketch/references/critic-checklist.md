# Critic checklist (binary — answer YES/NO per item, per image)

Used by the review loop (max 3 iterations). Any NO → fix spec or library, re-render, re-check.

1. PAPER — background reads as cream paper (plain or faint grid), not white or grey.
2. INK — structure is near-black felt-tip; strokes visibly hand-drawn (wobble, overshoot), not CAD-straight.
3. PRIMARY — every primary/solid button is diagonal-hatched; outline buttons are not.
4. PLACEHOLDERS — body copy is squiggles; images are X-boxes; add/empty states are dashed with "+".
5. LEGIBILITY — every real word is readable at 1x, including labels on hatched buttons.
6. NO COLLISIONS — no text overlaps a stroke, frame edge, or other text (except intentional strike-throughs).
7. CONTAINMENT — no component spills outside its screen/panel frame.
8. FLOW — arrows are curved/hand-drawn, start and end near the right elements, arrowheads visible.
9. ANNOTATIONS — margin notes are in lighter grey (or semantic red/green) handwriting with a leader line to what they describe.
10. ITERATION — where present, "vs." / CURRENT→NEW / strike-X + underlined verdict read unambiguously.
11. HIERARCHY — headings underlined and larger than body; screens aligned to a common top edge.
12. RESEMBLANCE — at a glance it could pass as a scanned page from a designer's notebook.
